M1 MAC TRANSFER - BUILD ON GITHUB, NOT YOUR MAC

Extract ChefsTouch-Source-Transfer.zip on Windows. Upload its contents,
including .github, to a private GitHub repository at the repository root.
Go to Actions > Build Logic Pro Audio Unit > Run workflow.
After a successful run download the Chefs-Touch-Logic-Pro-AU artifact.
On Mac extract the downloaded ZIP and the AU ZIP inside it.
Move Chefs Touch.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
(Create that folder if necessary.) Restart Logic or GarageBand.
Delete the downloaded archives after installation to recover space.
No Xcode, CMake, JUCE or source checkout is needed on your Mac.
The workflow builds universal arm64 + Intel AU and validates it.
The Mac build has not been run from this Windows workspace.
The component is ad-hoc signed, not Apple notarized.

22 presets: six original recipes plus Vocal and Master recipes for
Trap, Pop, Rock, R&B, Hip Hop, Acoustic, EDM, Jazz Soul.
Choose your song key and scale; adjust levels to the actual recording.
Mouth animation responds to vocal level; it does not recognize lyrics.
